Privacy notice

Khoj Control Room · last updated 28 October 2026

Khoj Control Room is an internal tool used by ShopDeck and Blitzscale staff to run Khoj. It is not offered to the public. This notice explains what it records about the people who use it.

What we collect about users

  • Sign-in: your work Google account's name and email address, as confirmed by Google when you sign in. We do not receive your Google password.
  • A session cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is essential for the tool to work; there are no advertising or third-party tracking cookies.
  • Usage: when you open the tool, which tabs you view and for how long, and the actions you take (for example ticking a task, changing a rule or the plan, refreshing data, downloading a CSV). Sign-in attempts from accounts outside the allowed domains are also logged.

Why

To control access to the tool, keep an audit trail of changes, and understand how the tool is used so it can be improved.

Business data shown in the tool

The tool displays ShopDeck business data from internal systems, including order and customer-level information. It is shown only to signed-in staff for running Khoj and is handled under ShopDeck's internal data policies.

Who can see it and where it is kept

Usage records are visible only to the tool's administrators. Data is hosted with our infrastructure providers (Netlify for hosting and storage; Google for sign-in). We do not sell or share it with anyone else.

How long

Usage records are kept for 180 days. Sessions expire after 30 days or when you sign out.
